# Orders and your account

## Creating an account
Click "Register" and enter your name, email address and a password with at least 8 characters.
You need an account to add items to your cart and to check out.

## Placing an order
Add products to your cart, open the cart and click "Checkout".
You can order up to 99 pieces of one product, as long as enough are in stock.

## Order status
After checkout, your order is "pending". It then becomes "processing", "shipped" and "delivered".
An order can also be "cancelled".

## Changing or cancelling an order
Orders can't be changed online after checkout. Please contact our support team as soon as possible.
