# Returns and refunds

## Return period
You can return most items within 30 days after delivery.
Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## How to return an item
Contact our support team with your order number. We will send you a return label.

## Refunds
After we receive the returned item, we refund the money within 5–7 business days.

## Items that can't be returned
For hygiene reasons, used yoga mats can't be returned unless they are faulty.
