# Shipping and delivery

## Where we deliver
We deliver to all countries in the European Union.

## Delivery times
Orders are usually shipped within 1–2 business days.
Delivery inside Germany takes 2–4 business days, and to other EU countries 4–7 business days.

## Shipping costs
Shipping is free for orders over €50. For smaller orders, shipping costs €4.90.

## Tracking your order
You can follow your order on the "Orders" page after logging in.
Its status changes from "pending" to "processing", then "shipped" and finally "delivered".
